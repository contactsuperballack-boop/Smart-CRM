1. What [PRODUCT NAME] is

A CRM that helps businesses track leads, draft follow-up emails with AI, send them from the user’s own Gmail account, and track replies.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and workspace details.
  • Business and lead data you enter: business profiles, and each lead’s name, email, phone, company, notes and status.
  • Email activity: the emails you approve and send through [PRODUCT NAME], and replies sent to the tracking address included in those emails (a Reply-To address on our domain).
  • Google account connection: if you connect Gmail, your Google email address and an OAuth refresh token (see section 3).
  • Technical data: basic logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) used for security and debugging.

3. Google user data

When you connect Gmail we request these permissions: (a) send email on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send), used only to send emails you have approved, and (b) your email address and basic identity (openid, email), used to show which account is connected and to set the From address. We do not request access to read, search, list, modify or delete messages in your mailbox, and we do not access your contacts or calendar.

We store the OAuth refresh token encrypted. We use it only to send emails you approve. You can disconnect at any time in the app; this revokes the token with Google and deletes it from our systems. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use. [PRODUCT NAME]’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train generalised AI models, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security or abuse investigation, or to comply with law.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the service: store your leads, draft and send emails you approve, capture replies, and create follow-up reminders.
  • To generate AI drafts and suggestions (see section 5).
  • To secure, maintain and improve the service, and to communicate with you about it.

5. AI processing

To draft emails and classify replies, we send the relevant business profile, lead details, prior email text and lead replies to our AI provider, Anthropic, through its API. Anthropic processes this content to generate the response. 7. Your leads’ data

You decide whom you contact. For the lead data you enter, you are the controller and we act as your processor. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to email your leads and for honouring unsubscribe requests. Each email includes an unsubscribe line; when a lead unsubscribes we stop sending to them.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete a lead or a business profile at any time. The account owner can delete the entire workspace in the app (Delete account): this removes the workspace’s data from our production systems straight away and revokes our access to your connected Gmail. You can also email us to request deletion, and we complete such requests within [30] days. Copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within [35] days, and copies held by our email-processing provider are removed under its retention schedule ([PROVIDER RETENTION PERIOD]). We keep only a minimal deletion record (an internal ID and counts, no personal data) to show that deletion happened.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Each customer’s data is logically isolated from others, and access is limited to people who need it.
